Privacy Policy

Hold'em Tally / Effective and last updated: November 8, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Hold'em Tally (for Android, iOS and the web; "the app") handles information about its users.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information. The developer never receives any information about you.

2. Information stored on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app stores the following information only on your own device:

  • The tournament in progress (the player names you enter, points, hand history and blind settings)
  • App settings (display language and whether the level-up sound is on)

This information never leaves your device, and the developer cannot see it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Choose "End this tournament" on the app's home screen to delete the tournament record
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything
  • On the web version, clear this site's data in your browser

3. Internet access and third-party services

The app does not connect to the internet. It does not use any third-party services such as advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

4. Device features (permissions)

The app uses the following features only for the purposes described. None of them asks you for permission.

  • Vibration: to let you know when the blinds go up
  • Audio settings: to play the level-up sound
  • Keeping the screen on: so the screen doesn't turn off during play

The app also includes a network permission that comes with the framework it is built on (React Native). The app does not use it to send or receive any data.

5. Sharing with third parties

Because the app collects no information, it shares none with third parties.

The stores you download the app from (Google Play and the App Store) handle information under their own privacy policies.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above. Before any major change to the information the app handles, such as adding advertising, notice will also be given in the app and on its store page.
